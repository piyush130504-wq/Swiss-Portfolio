CASE STUDY / 01

Rodic AI Checker

Live product ↗

A startup idea is often explained in fragments. The product creates a single starting point where a founder can frame that idea and begin an AI-assisted evaluation.

Focus
Idea evaluation experience
Contribution
Product development & UI implementation
Platform
Responsive web

The flow

01Arrive

Understand the product’s purpose at a glance.

02Describe

Provide an idea as a natural-language prompt.

03Evaluate

Move into an AI-assisted assessment.

Interface direction

Make the first step feel less intimidating.

The experience is organized around a prominent prompt and a clear action, reducing the distance between an initial idea and a useful next step. The interface is designed to hold up from desktop through mobile rather than treating responsive behaviour as an afterthought.

This is a product and engineering case study. It does not claim formal user research or design validation.

CASE STUDY / 02

REEP

Live product ↗

REEP needed to present a large set of industry challenges without making the exploration experience feel like a long, static list - while also bringing interested participants into a registration flow.

Focus
Problem-bank discovery & registration
Built with
React, FastAPI & MongoDB
Scale
53 problems / 14 sectors

The flow

01Explore

Scan a dynamic problem bank by sector.

02Narrow

Use animated filters and real-time interface feedback.

03Register

Complete a role-aware lead-registration flow.

Product decisions

Turn an extensive catalogue into a path to action.

Filtering and real-time UI interactions support quick scanning across 14 sectors. The registration experience includes email validation, role selection, and inline success handling - details that make a simple conversion path more legible and reassuring.

The deployed application was designed responsively for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.
